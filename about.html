Our tipuna

He kāwai whakapapa tēnei nō Raana Te Iti Walker rāua ko Mary Roa Milner.

The Incorporation carries the legacy of Raana Te Iti Walker and Mary Roa Milner — our tipuna whose connection to this whenua established the foundation for all that Tutumatai Station is today.

All descendants of Raana and Mary Roa are shareholders and eligible for the education grant programme. Their whakapapa is the thread that connects 78 owners across Aotearoa to this land in Tairāwhiti.
